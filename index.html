    Private by design

    Nothing you enter leaves your device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking: Heal keeps everything in this browser. You can export or delete your data at any time from Your impact.

    Use Heal like an app

    Add Heal to your home screen or desktop to open it in its own window, like any other app.

    ✓ Heal is saved in this browser, so it works offline too.

    No Install button? Look for Install or Add to Home Screen in your browser's menu. On an iPhone or iPad, it's under Share. Not every browser can install apps.

    On an iPhone or iPad, or from Safari on a Mac, the app keeps its own data, apart from your browser's. To bring your data across, export it from Your impact in your browser, then import it in the app.
